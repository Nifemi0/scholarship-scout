## Title

Scholarship Scout — WebMCP Scholarship Research Demo

## Description

Scholarship Scout is a source-transparent WebMCP scholarship research workspace for students and AI agents.

Scholarship Scout supports students across countries, study levels, and fields. In this one-minute demo, we use one Nigerian computer-science profile to search 30 curated opportunities, review explainable eligibility signals, compare scholarships, and generate a Mastercard Foundation application checklist. The production deployment exposes six typed WebMCP tools and keeps official provider sources visible so the student remains in control.

Live app: https://scholarship-scout-brown.vercel.app

Public source: https://github.com/Nifemi0/scholarship-scout

Built for The WebMCP Challenge 2026.
