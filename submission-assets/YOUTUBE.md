## Title

Scholarship Scout — WebMCP Scholarship Research Demo

## Description

Scholarship Scout is a source-transparent WebMCP scholarship research workspace for students and AI agents.

In this one-minute demo, a Nigerian computer-science profile searches 30 curated opportunities, reviews explainable eligibility signals, compares scholarships, and generates a Mastercard Foundation application checklist. The production deployment exposes six typed WebMCP tools and keeps official provider sources visible so the student remains in control.

Live app: https://scholarship-scout-brown.vercel.app

Public source: https://github.com/Nifemi0/scholarship-scout

Built for The WebMCP Challenge 2026.
